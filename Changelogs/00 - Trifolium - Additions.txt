--- Card Waves ---

In the config section there is an option to enable (#1 on by default) a series of card packages
with the objective of maintaining the parity of vanilla mechanics as mods are added to the game.

These packages will feature basic elements such as probability increases, card destruction, economy,
etc. And you may activate more or less as necessary or to your liking.

Wave #1 currently contains!:

-- Jokers --

"Clover" -- Rare -- 9 cost: Triples all listed probabilities.


-- Spectrals --

"Pit": Destroys the 3 highest ranked cards in hand. Gain 3 free rerolls this round.
