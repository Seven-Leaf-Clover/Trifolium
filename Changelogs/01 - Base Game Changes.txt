----- Stakes -----

-- Stake Rework --

In the config section of the mod there is an option to enable a small rework to the 
stakes with the objective of improving the game feeling as the difficulty scales.

It changes:

Blue Stake: Rework: Scoring scales faster: Last Ante starts at 400k Chips in Gold Stake.

New Stake: Emerald (above blue): +1 Shop slot

New Stake: Azure (Above orange): Spectral Packs appear x1.8 times often


-- Stake Expansion --

Also in the config section you can find an option to enable (on by default) the addition of two new
stakes that increase the difficulty of the rework.

It adds:

* Basic Blind refers to the Small and Big Blinds!

Yellow Stake: From Ante 5 until Win defeating a Baisc Blind permanently raises shop prices by $1.
 - This Stake resets and disables itself on Winning the game to allow for a better endless mode!

Gray Stake: Defeating a Basic Blind slightly increases the required score:

 - Given that the game's score scales based on an arbitrary pattern and not a percentage for example
   I will use ante 8 to explain for how much it ends up increasing. 
   
   On ante 8 assuming both the rework and expansion are enabled the Small Blind starts at 400k chips
   and every time you beat a Basic Blind with the Gray Stake enabled it will increase by 30k, after
   6 defeated Blinds, on the 7th it will start increasing by 40k and by the 15th it will start to
   increase by 50k.
   
   By the end of ante 6 or after 12 played Basic Blinds the game score requirement has doubled and
   if all 16 Blinds are played (in an 8 ante game) the score requirement would increase by 150%.
